# Phase 5 — Stability, objectives, collectibles, and scoring

Implement:
- Server-owned Simulation Stability starting at 100.
- Three stabilizer objectives across the arena.
- Data fragment spawning and one-time server-validated collection.
- Hold-to-repair interactions with range, interruption, and death handling.
- Separate team objective outcome and individual score.
- Score sources for survival, collection, repair/assist, and patch discovery.
- Results board and one-time rewards.

Clients may display progress but may not award score or stability. Test duplicate interactions, out-of-range requests, disconnects, interrupted repairs, repeated result callbacks, and tie/placement calculations.
